SERIAL - COM1 INITIALIZATION
File: kernel/init/serial.asm
Author: arfy slowy

OVERVIEW
Initializes the COM1 serial port during kernel boot.
Called after services start, before APIC and SMP init.

IMPLEMENTATION

kernel_init_serial:
  call driver_serial

Simply invokes the serial driver init routine to configure
COM1 at 115200 baud, 8N1, with FIFO enabled.
